Status pills

Text on its own tint, bounded by a 1px border in the status colour so the pill edge meets 3:1 on any surface. A filled dot marks a live or terminal state; a hollow dot marks waiting or inactive. Three sizes: sm for dense lists, md for panes, lg for customer and driver headers.

Map markers on tiles

Markers sit on OSM tiles, not on a system surface. Each gets a 2px white ring plus a dark edge so it holds 3:1 against land, road and water. Pickup is square, drop-off round, so shape carries kind as well as colour. A silent truck turns hollow, grey and dashed, and is always labelled.

Two traps

--ink-subtle fails 4.5:1 at body size. Use it at 18px+ or 14px bold+ only, never for text a person must read.

--divider fails 3:1. It separates list rows and nothing else. Any control boundary uses --border.
