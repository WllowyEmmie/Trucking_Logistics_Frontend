Confirm control: two candidates
Recommendation Ship A, press and hold

It meets SC 2.5.7 on its own: holding is not dragging, and one pointer does it. That keeps one control per screen.

A slide needs a steady horizontal drag, which is hard with gloves on a vibrating mount. Its required tap alternative then adds a second target and a second step, so the fast route is the hard one and the easy route is slow.

Hold is hard to trigger by brushing the screen, since a release under 0.8 s resets it, and it takes under a second on purpose. Keyboard and switch users hold Space or Enter. Screen reader users get a double-tap-and-hold, which VoiceOver and TalkBack both pass through as a long press.
